# Guides

::: applies
Firmware 1.2.0
:::

How to join a [[BBS]], how to set one up, and how to let people outside
your home in. Every guide is free to share and adapt, under
[CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), and lives
in [its own repository](https://github.com/rwmech/unleashed_documentation).

## Joining a board

- [Apps for joining](/docs/terminals): the free app you join a board with,
  on a phone, a computer or an old machine.
- [Your first call](/docs/firstcall): what happens when you connect, and
  what to type.
- [Making the dial links work](/docs/dialing): when you click an address
  and nothing happens.
- [The real risks of open communications](/docs/privacy): what travels in
  the clear, and what that means for you.

## Running a board

- [Set up your BBS](/docs/setup): five steps to a board online, then a
  guide for each part.
- [Let people outside your home join](/docs/forward): opening your board to
  the internet, with a guide for
  [ASUS](/docs/forward-asus), [NETGEAR](/docs/forward-netgear),
  [TP-Link](/docs/forward-tplink), [Xfinity](/docs/forward-xfinity) and
  [eero and Google Nest Wifi](/docs/forward-mesh).

## Adding to a board

- [Adding an SD card](/docs/sdcard): file areas, forums and screens of your
  own.
- [Lights](/docs/lights): a drive light and a strip of pixels for a board
  in a case.
- [Camera](/docs/camera): a board that can take a picture when a caller
  asks.
- [Skins](/docs/skins): dressing a board with a screen up as another
  machine.

## Bringing a new board

- [Bring your board](https://unleashedbbs.com/bring-your-board): porting a
  board that is not on the list yet, or building a sat of your own, and
  sending it in.

Putting the software on a board in the first place is on
[the installer page](https://unleashedbbs.com/install).
